What is PUSERVE?

PUSERVE (President University Serve Environment) is a student organization dedicated to real environmental and social action. We believe that small steps can create a massive impact for our planet and future generations.

What We Serve

Real Action

Executing tree planting, waste clean-ups, and ecosystem restoration for a sustainable future.

Ecological Education

Building ecological literacy and training environmentally conscious leaders through intensive programs.

Research & Innovation

Designing applicable solutions and action plans based on strong environmental data and observation.

Community Collaboration

Building cross-sector networks connecting students, local communities, NGOs, and governments.

Creative Advocacy

Spreading sustainability values through inclusive digital campaigns and interactive media.

Empowering Changemakers

Providing a dynamic platform for students to innovate and make a real-world impact.
