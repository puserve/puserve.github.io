**LLDIKTI Region IV’s 2026 “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program: A Collaboration Between PUSERVE and LLDIKTI Region IV for the 2026 “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program**

Fostering habits at the source for a cleaner West Java.

**About the Event**

The 2026 LLDIKTI Region IV “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program is a collaborative initiative between **PUSERVE (President University Serve Environment)** and **KEMDIKTISAINTEK (Ministry of Higher Education, Science, and Technology)** through **LLDIKTI Region IV**, supporting the implementation of the 2026 “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program. This activity will be held over 11–13 weeks.

**Background**

According to the program guidelines, waste generation in the Greater Bandung area exceeds 4,489 metric tons per day (2022–2023 study by the West Java Provincial Environmental Agency). It is estimated that only about 30% is managed adequately, while the remaining 70%—equivalent to approximately 3,500 metric tons per day—has not been optimally managed. In response to this situation, LLDIKTI Region IV has designated waste management as the priority theme for the 2026 Thematic KKN, with a target of involving approximately 25,000 students.

This program employs a social engineering approach: students act as motivators and catalysts for behavioral change in the community regarding waste sorting at the source, rather than as waste collectors or sorters.

**PUSERVE’s Role**

PUSERVE operates through two channels: hands-on field projects and forums for the exchange of ideas. Our mission is to educate students and the public about the environment and to collaborate with the government and local communities. In this initiative, PUSERVE serves as a collaborator to help ensure the event’s success.

**Let’s Take Action Together**

Environmental change starts with small habits at home. Together with PUSERVE and LLDIKTI Region IV, let’s turn our concern into real action.

**KKN Tematik Sampah Tuntas LLDIKTI Wilayah IV Tahun 2026: Kolaborasi PUSERVE x LLDIKTI Wilayah IV dalam KKN Tematik “Sampah Tuntas” 2026**

_Dari pembiasaan di hulu, untuk Jawa Barat yang lebih bersih._

**Tentang Event**

KKN Tematik Sampah Tuntas LLDIKTI Wilayah IV Tahun 2026 adalah kegiatan kolaborasi antara **PUSERVE (President University Serve Environment)** dan **KEMDIKTISAINTEK (Kementrian Pendidikan Tinggi, Sains dan Teknologi)** melalui **LLDIKTI Wilayah IV** yang mendukung pelaksanaan Program KKN Tematik “Sampah Tuntas” 2026. Kegiatan ini akan diselenggarakan **Selama 11-13 pekan** .

**Latar Belakang**

Menurut panduan program, timbulan sampah di kawasan Bandung Raya mencapai lebih dari 4.489 ton per hari (kajian DLH Provinsi Jawa Barat 2022/2023). Diperkirakan baru sekitar 30% yang tertangani secara memadai, sementara sekitar 70% sisanya, setara kurang lebih 3.500 ton per hari, belum tertangani optimal. Merespons kondisi ini, LLDIKTI Wilayah IV menjadikan pengelolaan sampah sebagai tema prioritas KKN Tematik 2026 dengan target pelibatan sekitar 25.000 mahasiswa.

Program ini memakai pendekatan rekayasa sosial: mahasiswa berperan sebagai motivator dan penggerak perubahan perilaku masyarakat dalam memilah sampah dari sumbernya, bukan sebagai petugas pengangkut atau pemilah sampah.

**Peran PUSERVE**

PUSERVE bergerak melalui dua jalur, yaitu proyek lapangan yang nyata dan forum pertukaran gagasan. Misi kami mengedukasi mahasiswa dan masyarakat tentang lingkungan serta berkolaborasi dengan pemerintah dan komunitas lokal. Dalam kegiatan ini, PUSERVE berperan sebagai kolaborator guna turut menyukseskan acara ini.
