**Cikarang, October 28, 2026 — PUSERVE (President University Serve Environment)** has officially established a collaboration with the **MoHEST (Ministry of Higher Education, Science, and Technology**) through active participation in the “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program (KKN) organized by LLDIKTI Region IV. This series of community service activities will take place intensively from October 2026 through December 2026, with the primary goal of addressing the urgent waste crisis in the Greater Bandung area and Sumedang Regency.

**Context of the Problem and Social Engineering Approach**

Based on a [study by the West Java Provincial Environmental Agency](https://kemdiktisaintek.go.id/news/article/kkn-tematik-sampah-tuntas-kemdiktisaintek-perkuat-peran-perguruan-tinggi-tangani-sampah-dari-hulu) (2026) using data from the 2024 National Waste Management Information System (SIPSN). The volume of waste generated in the Greater Bandung area has surpassed 4,489 metric tons per day. Approximately 70% of this amount—equivalent to 3,500 metric tons per day—has not been managed optimally and is overburdening the Final Disposal Site (TPA).

In response to this critical situation, the “Sampah Tuntas” Thematic Community Service Program aims to engage 25,000 University students from West Java and Banten Province. Unlike conventional approaches, this program employs social engineering methods. PUSERVE students are not deployed as waste collectors but rather act as motivators, facilitators, and catalysts for behavioral change among residents so they can independently manage their waste at the source.

**PUSERVE’s Central Role and Measurable Impact Goals**

In line with PUSERVE’s mission to promote an eco-friendly lifestyle through concrete actions, the PUSERVE delegation plays a strategic role as a facilitator in educating the public about waste sorting into four categories (organic, inorganic, hazardous waste, and residual waste) and the implementation of the 3R principles. This aligns with the organization’s philosophy that environmental issues are closely linked to community empowerment and well-being.

PUSERVE focuses on measurable outcomes, not merely superficial awareness-raising. Through this collaboration, PUSERVE aims for concrete outcomes, including a 15–20% reduction in residual waste sent to landfills, the establishment and institutional strengthening of neighborhood-level Waste Banks, communal composting facilities (such as Loseda, Biopori, or Black Soldier Fly maggot farming), and the development of environmental leaders who will ensure the sustainability of the program beyond the KKN period.

"Through KKNT Sampah Tuntas, we aim to turn environmental awareness into meaningful action and collective responsibility. We believe that a cleaner, more sustainable future begins with the choices we make today," said Edeline Stefanie, Chair of PUSERVE for the 2026–2027 term

The Head of LLDIKTI Region IV, Dr. Lukman, S.T., M.Hum., emphasized in his guidance: “Through this social engineering approach, we are shifting the paradigm of waste management. From an approach that was initially focused solely on end-of-line (downstream) management, we are now moving toward education and behavioral change starting at the source of household waste generation, embodying the spirit of GRADASI (Academic Collaboration, Synergy, and Innovation)."

**Series of Activities and Next Steps:**

A cultural shift in waste management requires ongoing support. Therefore, PUSERVE’s involvement will follow six structured phases over 12 weeks:

- Phases 0 & 1: Preparation, Observation, and Initial Mapping at the RW level (Weeks 1–3).
- Phase 2: Participatory Analysis and Development of an Action Plan with residents (Week 4).
- Phase 3: Action and Field Guidance, which form the core of the educational process (Weeks 5–8).
- Phase 4: Final Evaluation of Indicator Achievement and Reporting (Weeks 9–10).
- Phase 5: Implementation of the Model and Handover of the Management System to Community Leaders to Ensure Sustainability (Weeks 11–12).

**Next Steps: Making a Lasting Impact**

As part of its commitment to transparency and efforts to broaden the reach of inspiration, PUSERVE will periodically publish updates on the progress of each phase of this initiative. The general public and stakeholders can follow daily coverage, field observation results, and a gallery of students’ real-world actions through PUSERVE’s official website and social media channels.

This cross-sector collaboration is expected to be more than just a temporary community service program; rather, it is intended to serve as a catalyst for the emergence of a deeply rooted environmental culture among the community. PUSERVE believes that meaningful change always begins with concern that is translated into concrete action. Together, let’s build a society that not only enjoys nature but is also empowered to protect it.

**Cikarang, 28 Oktober 2026** — PUSERVE (President University Serve Environment) secara resmi menjalin kolaborasi dengan Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi, Sains, dan Teknologi (KEMDIKTISAINTEK) melalui partisipasi aktif dalam program Kuliah Kerja Nyata (KKN) Tematik "Sampah Tuntas" LLDIKTI Wilayah IV. Rangkaian kegiatan pengabdian masyarakat ini akan berlangsung secara intensif mulai Oktober 2026 hingga Desember 2026, dengan tujuan utama mengurai persoalan darurat sampah di kawasan Bandung Raya dan Kabupaten Sumedang.

**Konteks Persoalan dan Pendekatan Rekayasa Sosial** 

Berdasarkan [kajian Dinas Lingkungan Hidup Provinsi Jawa Barat ](https://kemdiktisaintek.go.id/news/article/kkn-tematik-sampah-tuntas-kemdiktisaintek-perkuat-peran-perguruan-tinggi-tangani-sampah-dari-hulu)(2026) berdasarkan Sistem Informasi Pengelolaan Sampah Nasional (SIPSN) 2024. Volume timbulan sampah di kawasan Bandung Raya telah menembus angka 4.489 ton per hari. Sekitar 70% dari jumlah tersebut—atau setara 3.500 ton per hari—belum tertangani secara optimal dan membebani Tempat Pemrosesan Akhir (TPA). 

Merespons kondisi kritis ini, program KKN Tematik "Sampah Tuntas" menargetkan pelibatan 25.000 mahasiswa Jawa Barat dan Provinsi Banten. Berbeda dengan pendekatan konvensional, program ini mengusung metode rekayasa sosial (_social engineering_). Mahasiswa PUSERVE tidak diterjunkan sebagai petugas pengangkut sampah, melainkan bertindak sebagai motivator, fasilitator, dan penggerak perubahan perilaku warga agar mampu mengelola sampahnya secara mandiri dari sumbernya.

**Peran Sentral PUSERVE dan Target Dampak Terukur**

Sesuai dengan misi PUSERVE untuk mendorong gaya hidup ramah lingkungan melalui aksi nyata, delegasi PUSERVE mengambil peran strategis sebagai fasilitator edukasi pemilahan empat kategori sampah (Organik, Anorganik, B3, dan Residu) serta penerapan prinsip 3R. Hal ini sejalan dengan filosofi organisasi bahwa isu lingkungan sangat erat kaitannya dengan pemberdayaan dan kesejahteraan masyarakat.

PUSERVE berfokus pada hasil yang terukur (_tangible outcomes_), bukan sekadar peningkatan kesadaran di permukaan. Dalam kolaborasi ini, PUSERVE menargetkan luaran nyata berupa pengurangan 15-20% sampah residu ke TPA, inisiasi dan penguatan kelembagaan Bank Sampah tingkat RW, fasilitas pengomposan komunal (seperti Loseda, Biopori, atau budi daya Maggot BSF), serta lahirnya kader-kader lingkungan yang akan memastikan keberlanjutan program purna-KKN.

_"Melalui KKNT Sampah Tuntas, kami berupaya mengubah kesadaran lingkungan menjadi tindakan yang bermakna dan tanggung jawab bersama. Kami percaya bahwa masa depan yang lebih bersih dan berkelanjutan dimulai dari pilihan-pilihan yang kita buat hari ini,_" ujar Edeline Stefanie, Ketua PUSERVE Periode 2026-2027.

Kepala LLDIKTI Wilayah IV, Dr. Lukman, S.T., M.Hum., dalam panduan arahannya menegaskan, _"Melalui pendekatan rekayasa sosial ini, kita melakukan pergeseran paradigma penanganan sampah. Dari yang awalnya hanya berorientasi pada pengelolaan di titik akhir (hilir), kini menuju edukasi dan pembiasaan perilaku sejak dari sumber timbulan sampah di rumah tangga, mewujudkan semangat GRADASI (Gotong Royong Akademisi Bersinergi dan Berinovasi)."_

**Rangkaian Kegiatan dan Langkah Selanjutnya:**

Perubahan budaya pengelolaan sampah membutuhkan pendampingan berkelanjutan. Oleh karena itu, keterlibatan PUSERVE akan mengikuti 6 (enam) fase terstruktur selama 12 minggu:

- **Fase 0 & 1:** Persiapan, Observasi, dan Pemetaan Awal di tingkat RW (Minggu ke-1 s.d. ke-3).
- **Fase 2:** Analisis Partisipatif dan Penyusunan Rencana Aksi bersama warga (Minggu ke-4).
- **Fase 3:** Aksi dan Pendampingan Lapangan yang menjadi inti edukasi (Minggu ke-5 s.d. ke-8).
- **Fase 4:** Evaluasi Akhir pencapaian indikator dan Pelaporan (Minggu ke-9 s.d. ke-10).
- **Fase 5:** Implementasi Model dan serah terima sistem pengelolaan kepada kader agar berkelanjutan (Minggu ke-11 s.d. ke-12).

**Langkah Selanjutnya: Mengukir Dampak yang Berkelanjutan** 

Sebagai komitmen terhadap transparansi dan upaya memperluas jangkauan inspirasi, PUSERVE akan mempublikasikan perkembangan progresif dari setiap fase kegiatan ini secara berkala. Seluruh masyarakat dan pemangku kepentingan dapat memantau artikel liputan harian, hasil observasi lapangan, serta galeri aksi nyata mahasiswa melalui situs web dan kanal media sosial resmi PUSERVE.

Kolaborasi lintas sektor ini diharapkan tidak hanya usai sebagai program pengabdian sementara, melainkan menjadi katalisator lahirnya budaya lingkungan yang mengakar kuat di tengah warga. PUSERVE percaya bahwa perubahan yang bermakna selalu berawal dari kepedulian yang diwujudkan dalam aksi nyata. Bersama-sama, mari kita wujudkan masyarakat yang tidak hanya menikmati alam, tetapi juga berdaya melindunginya.
